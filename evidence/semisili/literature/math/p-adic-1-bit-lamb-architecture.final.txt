Distributed Optimization Bottlenecks and the
Mechanics of 1-Bit LAMB
Distributed optimization of large-scale deep neural networks is fundamentally constrained by
network communication overhead
1
. As foundation models expand to hundreds of billions of
parameters, distributed data-parallel training rapidly transitions from a compute-bound
workload to a network bandwidth-bound workload
2

dynamically adapting learning rates across distinct layers based on the ratio between the
Euclidean norm of the model weights and the norm of the adaptive update vector

floating-point gradient tensors across cluster interconnects during collective AllReduce
operations

Reimagining deep learning processors through non-Archimedean mathematics addresses the
fundamental physical and architectural bottlenecks that limit distributed scale

Mapping 1-bit LAMB onto a native 2-adic processing architecture establishes deep structural
