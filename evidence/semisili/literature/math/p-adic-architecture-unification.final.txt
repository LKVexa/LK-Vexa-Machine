Moy-Prasad Filtration and Representation Theory
To fully comprehend the structural rigor of the Bruhat-Tits building and its applicability to

subgroup G_{x,0}, known as a parahoric subgroup, which acts as the stabilizer of the point x up

In the 1990s, Allen Moy and Gopal Prasad drastically advanced this framework by establishing
a continuous filtration of these parahoric subgroups by smaller normal subgroups, denoted

of the group.

This filtration acts as a mathematical sieve, classifying p-adic representations by relating them
